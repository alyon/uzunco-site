CaseDoc Terms of Use

What the app is

CaseDoc is a case records app for surgeons: it helps you keep patients, cases, photos and notes organised. It is not a medical device and offers no diagnosis, treatment advice or clinical decision support. Clinical decisions and the accuracy of your records are yours.

Your data

Records are stored only on your device. There is no server; UZUNCO LTD cannot access your records and cannot recover them if they are lost. If you lose the device, delete the app, or forget your passcode and recovery code, the data may become inaccessible.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping the app passcode and recovery code safe; making regular backups; giving your patients the required information and obtaining their consent, and complying with applicable data protection rules such as GDPR / KVKK; and keeping your device secure (screen lock, updates).

Subscription

A subscription is bought and managed through the App Store or Google Play; payment, cancellation and refunds follow those stores’ rules. On iOS, Apple’s standard license agreement (EULA) applies.
