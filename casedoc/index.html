CaseDoc

A clinical notebook for surgeons, from the first consultation to the last follow-up.

What it does

  • Patient records with a structured clinical form (history, examination, operation plan).
  • Operation notes as text, voice recordings or a photo of a handwritten note.
  • Pre-operative, design and post-operative photos, and consent documents.
  • Follow-up dates with reminders on the phone.
  • Print or share a case as PDF.
  • Encrypted backup that only the doctor can open.
  • App lock with passcode and, optionally, Face ID or fingerprint.

Privacy

Everything the doctor enters stays on the doctor’s device. CaseDoc has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. Data leaves the phone only when the doctor exports or shares it.
