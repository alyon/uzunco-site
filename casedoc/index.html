One case, start to finish

Five moments in a case, and where each one lives in CaseDoc.

01 · Consultation

A structured clinical form

History, examination and operation plan, answered section by section. The draft saves itself as you go.

02 · Photos and consents

Photos and consents, filed under the case

Pre-operative, design and post-operative photos sit in the case, next to the consent documents. Anything still missing is flagged on the case.

03 · Operation

The operative note, your way

Type it, record it by voice, or photograph your handwritten note. Leave it as a draft and finish it later.

04 · Follow-up

Follow-ups that remind you

Set the follow-up dates and your phone reminds you on the day. Unwritten operative notes get a reminder too.

05 · Paper

Print it, or share it as a PDF

The pre-op analysis form and the whole case print cleanly, or go out as a PDF when you decide.

Privacy

Your records stay on your phone.

Everything you enter stays on your own device. Data leaves the phone only when you export or share it.

  • No account, no serverYou never sign up, and we run no server for records to be sent to.
  • Encrypted backupThe backup is encrypted, and only you can open it.
  • App lockA passcode, plus Face ID or fingerprint if you want it.
  • No ads, no analyticsNothing in the app tracks how you use it.
