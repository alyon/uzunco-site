CaseDoc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 05.10.2026 · Version: 1.0

This policy explains how the CaseDoc mobile app for iOS and Android (formerly KlinikDefter) handles information. The app is developed by UZUNCO LTD (registered in England and Wales, company number 17258456) ("we"). Contact: aliuzun.it@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • CaseDoc is a clinical notebook in which physicians keep their own patient records. It works entirely on the device.
  • There is no account, sign-in or server. The app does not send data over the internet.
  • The patient information you enter is stored only on your device. We cannot access it.
  • There are no ads, no analytics (usage tracking) and no third-party tracking software.
  • Information leaves the device only through an action you start: a backup file, a PDF printout, sharing a file, or a feedback e-mail.

2. Information handled in the app

You enter the following information, and it is stored on your device:

  • Patient identity and contact details: first and last name, date of birth, nationality, occupation, phone number.
    • The Turkish national ID number (TC Kimlik No) is not stored in full. Only a one-way hash and its last 4 digits are kept.
  • Health data (special category personal data): clinical form answers, operation plans and notes, comorbidities, medications and allergies, follow-up dates.
  • Images and audio: patient photos and videos, consent documents (photo / PDF), voice recordings of operation notes, analysis drawings.
  • Administrative notes: operation fee and payment status, hospital and team details. These are notes only; the app does not take payments.
  • App settings: language, lock timeout, opening picture.
    • The opening picture is visible on the lock screen. Do not choose a patient photo.
  • Lock data: only a salted hash of your app passcode, kept in the device's secure key store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
    • If you use fingerprint or Face ID, the operating system performs the check. Your biometric data never reaches the app.
  • Error log: a short list of the app's recent warnings and errors (at most 200 lines).
    • This log contains no patient information. It holds only the name of the part of the app, a fixed error description and an error code.
    • It stays on the device and is not included in backups.

3. Where and how information is stored

  • Information is stored in the app's own storage area, which the operating system protects. Other apps cannot read it.
  • iPhone: iOS Data Protection applies. App data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backup; those backups are under the control of your Apple account. The hash of the app passcode is not included in them.
  • Android: Google's automatic app backup is turned off. Patient data is not backed up to Google Drive automatically.
    • On Android, screenshots are blocked by default and app content is hidden in the recent apps screen. Both can be allowed in Settings.
  • The app lock (a passcode of 6 or more digits) is mandatory. The app locks itself after the time you set.

4. Information leaving the device (only through your action)

Action What leaves Protection
Back up All records, as one .kdyedek file Encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password you choose. If the password is forgotten, the file cannot be opened — not by us either.
Print / Share PDF The form or photos of the case you select Not encrypted. Keeping it safe at the destination you choose is your responsibility.
Export all records Forms as PDF and voice recordings, plus, if you choose, the original photos and videos and the consent documents, in a zip with files named after the patient Not encrypted.
Share a photo / video / voice recording The single file you select Not encrypted. For voice recordings, the app warns you before sharing.
Report a problem / Send a request An e-mail from your own e-mail app: the text you write, the app version, operating system and device model. The error log is added only if you choose (you are asked each time). Your e-mail provider's security.

You choose where a file goes (Files, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, e-mail, messaging, etc.). Copies stored in those services are subject to their own terms.

5. Information we receive

  • We receive only the e-mails you choose to send us. They contain your e-mail address, what you wrote, the version and device details and, if you added it, the error log.
  • We use this information only to resolve your issue and improve the app. We do not sell or share it.
  • E-mails are kept for at most 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Please do not include patient information (name, national ID, phone, photos) in e-mails.

6. App permissions

  • Camera: patient photos and videos, consent photos. Requested only when you tap the camera button.
  • Microphone: voice recordings of operation notes. Recording happens only when you start it, and continues if the screen locks.
  • Notifications: follow-up and operation-note reminders.
    • Reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is used.
    • The notification text contains only counts and patient initials (e.g. "A. A."), never full names.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / fingerprint): optional unlocking.
  • Photo library: the system photo picker is used. The app accesses only the photos you select, not your whole library.
  • Location, contacts, calendar and the advertising ID are not used.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Deleting a patient: the patient and all their cases, photos, consents, notes and voice recordings are permanently deleted, after you confirm with the app passcode.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all app data on the device. On iPhone, earlier iCloud or computer backups and any files you exported must be deleted separately.
  • Deleted data remains in any backup you made earlier.

8. Data protection law (KVKK) and responsibility

  • For the patient data entered into CaseDoc, the data controller is the physician or healthcare organisation using the app.
  • Informing patients, obtaining any required consent, and complying with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and health regulations are the responsibility of the physician or organisation.
  • We do not access or process patient data. The app is a tool that runs only on the physician's device.
  • When you e-mail us, we are the data controller for the information in that e-mail (section 5).
    • To exercise your rights under KVKK Article 11 or the UK GDPR (access, correction, deletion, etc.), write to aliuzun.it@gmail.com. In the UK you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
    • E-mails are stored on the servers of the e-mail service we use, which may be located outside Turkey.

9. Who may use the app

CaseDoc is intended for physicians and healthcare professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes

  • We may update this policy. The current version is published on this page with its effective date.
  • If a feature that collects data is ever added to the app, it will be clearly announced and optional. Examples: automatic crash reports, cloud backup, subscriptions.
