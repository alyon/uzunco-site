What you know before work starts

  1. We talk first.

    You describe the situation; we ask and listen. If your job is not a fit for us, we say so.

  2. We write the needs down, point by point.

    What exists, what is missing and what goes into the first version, in one list.

  3. The quote shows what is not included.

    Anything we don't know yet, or don't do, is written in the quote. You see the price before work starts, with no surprise items later.

  4. We deliver in pieces.

    Working parts tested on real devices, instead of one big delivery months later. Every change is noted in words you can read.

  5. We're still here after launch.

    App Store, Google Play or store launch, then fixes and updates.

Questions

If your question is not here, write to us; we reply within two working days. Write to us →

How is the price set?

After we write the needs down point by point, we give a fixed quote that lists what is included. You see the price before work starts. For one-problem jobs, we give you a cap before we start, and the bill does not go over it. Quotes are free: we don't charge for a quote you don't accept.

Who owns the code?

The code and the accounts (App Store, Google Play, Shopify) for work we do for you are in your name.

Can I write about just one problem?

Yes. A store rejection, a single bug or a second opinion is a job type of its own for us.

Can you continue an app someone else built?

Yes. We review the code first and tell you what state it is in and what to do first.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. App Store and Google Play submission, privacy forms and the review process are on us.

Do you use AI?

Yes, and we say so. A person approves every change, and your customers' data does not go into AI tools without your permission.

Where are you based?

UZUNCO LTD is registered in England and Wales. We work remotely, in Turkish and English.

Have a project in mind?

You don't need a spec, a budget or a tech decision. Tell us what it is in your own words, a whole project or one problem. We reply within two working days.
